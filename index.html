What can ScanIt read?

  • QR codes on Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID and ration cards (where QR is present)
  • Ayushman Bharat card QR codes
  • School registration, marksheets, certificates and admit cards—text OCR from uploaded images
  • Other QR codes, links, plain text and supported barcode-like QR payloads
Privacy note: ScanIt does not upload or save your images. QR data is decoded in your browser. OCR is performed locally using a browser-side OCR engine. Some official QR codes (such as Aadhaar secure QR) may contain encrypted or signed data that cannot be fully interpreted by a generic scanner.
